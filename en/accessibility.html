Accessibility Statement

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This is a translation of the Hebrew statement. If the two differ, the Hebrew version prevails.

Our commitment

We care about making the service accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities, in line with Israel’s Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law and Israeli Standard 5568.

Level of accessibility

The app partially conforms to accessibility requirements. A full, comprehensive accessibility audit against WCAG 2.0 level AA has not been done yet.

What we’ve implemented

  • Roles and descriptions for tappable elements (buttons, checkboxes) to support the VoiceOver screen reader.
  • Visible labels for all main form fields.
  • Text descriptions for icon-only buttons.
  • Text scales with the device’s font size setting.
  • Full right-to-left (RTL) support for Hebrew, and left-to-right for English.

Known limitations

Some elements may not yet be fully tested with every screen reader. The ads shown before sorting by aisle come from Google AdMob and aren’t under our control, so some of them may not be fully accessible. If an ad keeps you from sorting, contact us. We keep working to improve accessibility.
