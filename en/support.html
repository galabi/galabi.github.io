Support

A question, a problem or an idea? Write to us: galabisira@gmail.com

How do I share a list?

In the list: Settings → Send invite link, and send it to whoever you like (on WhatsApp, for example). You can also add a member by their tag (#…) or username.

Are my lists backed up?

A list you haven’t shared is stored only on your phone. A shared list is also stored on the server, so all its members see the same list.

Do I need to sign up?

No. An account is created automatically on first launch, with no email or password. Lists work offline too.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Your account and every list you own are deleted permanently.

Why is there an ad before sorting by aisle?

Sorting uses AI that costs us money, and the ad pays for it. The rest of the app has no ads.
