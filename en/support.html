Support

A question, a problem or an idea? Write to us: supermarketlist@mail.com

How do I share a list?

In the list: Settings → add a member, by the tag (#…) or username of the person you want to share with. Everyone finds their tag in the app’s Settings.

Do I need to sign up?

No. An account is created automatically on first launch, with no email or password. Lists work offline too.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. Your account and every list you own are deleted permanently.

Why is there an ad before sorting by aisle?

Sorting uses AI that costs us money, and the ad pays for it. The rest of the app has no ads.
