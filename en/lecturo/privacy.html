Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short

Lecturo has no server and no sign-up, and it sends no data to us. Everything you enter (courses, attendance and assignments) stays on your phone. When the app shows ads, Google AdMob collects device and usage data, as described under "Third-party services".

What is stored, and where

Your semesters, courses, schedule, attendance, holidays and assignments are stored in a local database inside the app, on your device only. None of it is sent to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

If you back up your device (to iCloud or a computer), the app's data is included in that backup according to your settings and Apple's privacy policy.

Your ad consent choices are also stored on the device.

Notifications

Assignment reminders and the daily catch-up reminder are local notifications scheduled by your phone. They don't pass through any server. You can allow or turn them off at any time in Settings → Notifications → Lecturo.

Third-party services

Google AdMob: when the app shows ads, they come from Google AdMob. Google collects device and usage data (such as IP address, device type and interaction with the ad) to show and measure ads. Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is collected only if you allowed tracking in the iOS prompt, and you can turn that off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Users in the European region are asked for consent first. What you enter in the app is never sent to AdMob. More: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Apart from that, the app contains no analytics or tracking.

Children

The app is meant for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete a semester, course or assignment inside the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
