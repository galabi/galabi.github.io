How do I get started?

In the Schedule tab: create a semester with a start and end date, add your courses, and give each course its weekly classes (day, time and room). Lecturo creates every class until the end of the semester.

A class falls on a holiday. Will it count as missed?

No. Add the holiday or break to the semester in the Schedule tab, and classes on those days are cancelled on their own.

I changed my schedule mid-semester. What happens to my attendance?

The attendance you already marked stays, even if you moved a class to another day.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Lecturo. Assignment reminders arrive 24 hours before the deadline and at 8:00 on the due day. The catch-up reminder arrives at 20:00 while there are classes to catch up on.

Is my data backed up?

Your data is stored only on your phone, and it's included in your device backup if you have one turned on. There's no sync between devices.
